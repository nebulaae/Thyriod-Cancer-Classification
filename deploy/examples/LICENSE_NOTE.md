
**Example cases.** The two example patients are taken from the public dataset of Y.-Y. Pang *et al.*,
"Construction of a Multimodal Machine Learning Model for Papillary Thyroid Carcinoma Based on Pathomics
and Ultrasound Radiomics Data", Mendeley Data, V1, doi:[10.17632/dp7zp2pb2v.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/dp7zp2pb2v.1),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0). Changes: cytology blocks converted
from TIFF to PNG. The dataset authors do not endorse this demo.
The examples were part of the training data of the deployed model, so they illustrate the interface and are not an independent test.
